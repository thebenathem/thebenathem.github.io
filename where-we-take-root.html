Where We

THE WORLDS THAT ANSWER · BOOK ONE

Hale finds joy in growing living things aboard an isolated colony base. Thrayn hears the harmonies that connect all life.

When their worlds meet, language comes slowly: look, wait, help, stay. Trust grows in shared work, small kindnesses and the decision to keep trying when understanding would be easier to abandon.

Where We Take Root is a quiet queer science-fiction romance about tending what survives and choosing where—and with whom—a life can begin again.
