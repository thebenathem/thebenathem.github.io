The Distance We Keep Open · Book One

What Safety
Allows

Eli Dacre has learned that every offer of safety comes with a price.

After years of forced labor, medical coercion and experiments that altered his nervous system, Eli escapes his captors in a damaged maintenance pod. He expects to die alone and free.

Instead, rescue brings him face to face with another choice: whether safety can ever be real when accepting it means trusting someone else.
