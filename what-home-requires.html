COMING SOON

The Distance We Keep Open · Book Two

What Home
Requires

Eight months after choosing a life together, Eli and Niran have built something neither expected: a home aboard the Tern.

When evidence of missing Span-sensitive people leads them to an abandoned gateway, Eli sees a chance to reconnect an isolated settlement before its life-support systems fail.

But reopening the Span will test more than Eli's altered nervous system. If their relationship is truly equal, Niran must accept that love does not give him the authority to forbid Eli's risks.
